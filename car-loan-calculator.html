Car Loan Calculator

About this calculator

On-road price, down payment and trade-in worked through to a monthly payment — and the total cost of the car once interest is counted.

How to use it
  • Trade-in value is applied against the amount financed, alongside the down payment.
  • Car loan terms are short by design; the calculator caps at eight years because longer terms rarely make sense.
  • Total cost of the car adds your down payment to everything you will repay.
  • A car depreciates while the loan runs, so a long term can leave you owing more than the vehicle is worth.

Questions

How long should a car loan be?

Shorter than the useful life of the car — three to five years for most buyers. Longer terms reduce the monthly figure but risk negative equity.

Does a trade-in reduce my interest?

Yes, indirectly. It lowers the amount financed, so interest accrues on a smaller balance.

Should I finance the on-road price or the ex-showroom price?

On-road, which includes registration, insurance and taxes. Financing only the ex-showroom price understates what you actually need.
