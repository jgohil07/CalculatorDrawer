Compound Interest Calculator

About this calculator

Growth of a deposit at any compounding frequency, with the effective annual yield that lets you compare offers on equal terms.

Formula
A = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × t
P = principal · r = annual rate · n = compounding periods per year · t = years

How to use it
  • Compounding frequency matters: the same nominal rate yields more the more often it compounds.
  • Effective annual yield converts any nominal rate and frequency into one comparable number.
  • The chart separates principal from accumulated interest year by year.
  • A nominal 7.5% compounded monthly is really 7.763% a year — that is the figure to compare against other products.

Questions

What is the effective annual yield?

The rate that would produce the same growth if compounded once a year. It is the only fair way to compare products with different compounding frequencies.

Does daily compounding make much difference?

Less than people expect. Moving from monthly to daily on a 7.5% rate adds only a few hundredths of a percentage point.

What is the rule of 72?

Dividing 72 by the annual rate gives a rough doubling time — about 9.6 years at 7.5%. It is an approximation that works well for moderate rates.
