About this calculator

How long a balance takes to clear at a fixed payment — and a clear warning when the payment does not even cover the monthly interest.

How to use it

  • Card APR is charged monthly on the outstanding balance, which is why balances grow so quickly.
  • If your payment is below the first month's interest, the balance never clears — the calculator says so and shows the minimum that would.
  • Total interest is often a large fraction of the balance itself at typical card rates.
  • Raising the payment even slightly shortens the payoff dramatically, because more of each payment hits principal.

Questions

Why does paying the minimum take so long?

Minimum payments are usually set just above the monthly interest, so very little goes to principal. A balance can take decades to clear this way.

What APR do credit cards charge?

Commonly 30–45% annualised on revolving balances, far above any other consumer credit. This is why card debt is usually the first thing to clear.

Is a balance transfer worth it?

Often, if the promotional rate is genuinely lower and you can clear the balance before it expires. Factor in the transfer fee.
